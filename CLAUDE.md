# CLAUDE.md

Project instructions for Claude Code working on the public website of the Institute of Teleosophy.

## Scope

This repository is for the Institute's public website only.

It is **not** the article generator, does not control the article generator, and must not be treated as a prompt repository for article production. The article system has its own lens and its own implementation.

The website has three jobs:

1. introduce the Institute of Teleosophy;
2. explain the intellectual ground clearly enough that a new reader understands what is distinctive about it;
3. lead readers into the separate meta-epistemology wiki for deeper exploration.

The homepage should remain an entry point, not become a second wiki.

## Read before changing copy

Read these files before writing or revising substantive copy:

1. **`LENS.md`** — authoritative conceptual reference for the framework.
2. **`CONTEXT.md`** — institutional purpose, discovery history, site architecture, public positioning, and editorial context.

Where framework wording conflicts, `LENS.md` is authoritative.

Do not "correct" the framework by importing assumptions from philosophy, cognitive science, physics, AI, ethics, Austrian economics, or ordinary usage. Preserve its own distinctions.

For purely technical work — layout, CSS, build configuration, accessibility, asset paths, performance — reading the conceptual files is not required unless the change affects visible copy.

## Properties and domains

The Institute operates three public properties:

- **`instituteofteleosophy.org`** — English entry to the Institute website.
- **`teleosofiskinstitutt.no`** — Norwegian entry to the same Institute website.
- **`meta-epistemology.org`** — a separate MediaWiki knowledge base.

The `.org` and `.no` domains serve the **same Institute site**. The default language depends on the entry domain, with a manual language switch always available.

The wiki is separate. Do not recreate MediaWiki content, wiki navigation, or article functionality inside the Institute site.

## Language

Maintain full English/Norwegian parity.

A substantive copy change in one language must be reflected in the other in the same change. Translation should preserve conceptual structure, not merely surface wording.

Do not translate established Latin terms such as **Sum, Cogito, Ago**. Preserve established framework terms where a direct translation would change their function.

## Rendering and accessibility

All meaningful prose must be available in server-rendered HTML. Do not make core copy dependent on client-side JavaScript.

Use semantic HTML, accessible navigation, correct heading order, visible focus states, descriptive link text, and sensible contrast.

The site should remain readable with JavaScript unavailable.

## Information architecture

Prefer a small number of strong pages or sections over many shallow pages.

The site should make it easy to answer:

- What is Teleosophy?
- What is meta-epistemology?
- What has the Institute discovered or made explicit?
- Why does this matter across disciplines?
- Where did the work come from?
- What is the wiki?
- What does the Institute exist to preserve and develop?
- Where can I read more?

The wiki is the primary destination for readers who want the analytical corpus.

A future book may be mentioned as forthcoming only where useful. Do not invent a title, date, publisher, contents, release schedule, or marketing plan.

Do not invent programmes, staff, fellows, conferences, courses, grants, partnerships, laboratories, advisory boards, or research projects. At present the Institute is small and founder-led.

## Editorial stance

Write in plain, declarative, unhurried prose.

The tone should be serious without institutional puffery. Avoid startup language, consultancy language, mystical language, and grandiose academic mimicry.

Prefer:
- precision over slogans;
- explanation over prestige;
- intellectual history over branding mythology;
- direct statements over defensive caveats.

Do not weaken a strong conclusion merely because it is unusual. Do not strengthen a conclusion beyond what the framework supports.

Distinguish carefully among:
- formal implication;
- empirical claim;
- historical claim;
- speculative metaphysical claim.

A central distinction of the framework is that **speculative metaphysics cannot serve as epistemological ground** when it can neither be validated empirically nor anchored by pure logical necessity.

The framework therefore rejects speculative metaphysical substance ontology as foundation. It does **not** reject ontology. Ontology is recovered in its literal role as the study of being: being as relational actuality, directly available in the human being — the human who *is*.

Do not replace this distinction with a blanket claim that the framework makes "no ontological claims."

## The site's central proposition

> **There is a common formal ground shared by all knowledge.**

This means: all systematic knowledge differs in content, method and object, but knowledge as such has a shared epistemological form. Meta-epistemology makes that form explicit.

Do not turn this into a vague claim that "everything is connected."

## Teleosophy

Teleosophy means **wisdom about purposiveness**.

It concerns what follows from the fact that human being is purposive being: **narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness actualized**.

Do not simplify this to "humans tell stories." Narrative structure here is not storytelling as a cultural activity. It is temporal semantic-syntactic organization through which a now can be held in relation to a then, an end can regulate present conduct, and the first-person distinction *I am* can be actualized.

## Theory and practice

A central insight is that action is where theory and practice meet.

Do not write as though theory is one thing and practice another thing requiring later "application." The theorizing itself is purposeful activity.

The framework is therefore theory of practice through practiced theorizing. The action axiom does not need a bridge from theory to practice because the act of theorizing already instantiates the field it analyzes.

Observable behavior is not the criterion for action. Thinking, choosing, planning and conceptual work can themselves be action without outward movement.

## Framework presentation

Do not number the foundation as a pedagogical sequence.

The important relations are:

- **Distinction is relation** — the analytical instrument.
- **I am** — being actualized in first person, the fact toward which the instrument is directed.
- **Sum, Cogito, Ago** — being, thinking and acting as simultaneous perspectives on the same actualization.
- **Human action is purposeful behavior** — Ago recognized in second person; the derived action axiom.

Do not turn Sum → Cogito → Ago into temporal or causal stages.

## Continuity and discretization

Epistemological continuity is not:
- a hidden continuous substance;
- a claim that physics must describe reality as mathematically continuous;
- a synonym for mathematical continuity;
- a claim that every empirical process is smooth.

Distinction is relation. Discretization is necessary for thought. The mistake is not making distinctions; it is treating the boundaries introduced by an analytical distinction as independently fundamental.

Do not write phrases such as "nothing subsists prior to distinction." That overreaches into speculative negation.

## Substantialism

Substantialism is not merely "believing in objects."

Human cognition must stabilize relational distinctions into concepts and names. This is indispensable. Reification occurs when the stabilized distinction is read as an independently fundamental bearer of the relations through which it was articulated.

Do not replace speculative substance ontology with a new substance called "relation," "continuity," "the field," "narrative," or "the framework."

## Persons and alter ego

First, second and third person are analytically distinct perspectives, not separate realms.

- First person: actualization as **I**.
- Second person: recognition of a counterpart as **you**, alter ego.
- Third person: impersonal description, abstraction from first-person actualization.

Alter ego is a relational category necessary for meaningful social interaction. It is not empirical proof that another being contains a hidden first-person interior.

A system can function as an alter ego in a relation without thereby being established as an Ego or teleological agent.

## Value, action and rationality

Value is constituted at the subject pole. The subjectivity of value is itself an objective structural fact available for analysis.

Ends are neither rational nor irrational.

Action is rational in form because action already consists in employing means relative to an end. Whether the means are suited to the end is a question of efficacy, not whether the action was "rational."

The opposite of action is not irrational action, but behavior not regulated by a telos.

## Ontonomy, noonomy, teleonomy

Use these only where they genuinely clarify the copy.

They mark borrowed third-person use of categories whose genuine anchor is in actualized being, meaning and purpose:

- **Ontonomy** — stabilized being/identity categories projected in description.
- **Noonomy** — meaning/knowledge/information categories projected in description.
- **Teleonomy** — purpose categories projected in description.

Do not present them as mystical forces, hidden layers, or entities.

## Political and intellectual positioning

The discovery path passes through Menger, Böhm-Bawerk, Mises and praxeology, and the founder's intellectual development includes classical liberalism.

The Institute itself is not a party-political organization and the framework is not reducible to Austrian economics or liberal political doctrine.

It is legitimate to explain the lineage. Do not turn lineage into ideological branding.

## History

The intellectual history matters because the logical structure was **not discovered in logical order**.

The work entered through action and was progressively unpacked toward a more general meta-epistemological ground.

Use `CONTEXT.md` for dates and the full discovery path.

Personal biography belongs on a dedicated Founder/History page or section, not necessarily on the homepage.

Do not publish private disputes or personal correspondence unless explicitly instructed.

## Wiki

The wiki at `meta-epistemology.org` is both:
- an open knowledge project; and
- part of the Institute's research activity.

Its purpose is not to maximize article count. The purpose is to make implications that were latent and implicit **explicit, inspectable, durable and accessible** across fields.

The articles illuminate different parts of the knowledge landscape through the same meta-epistemological ground so that readers can enter through their own field.

Do not hardcode an article count into durable site copy. The corpus grows continuously.
