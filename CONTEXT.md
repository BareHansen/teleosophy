## 1. The Institute

The **Institute of Teleosophy / Teleosofisk Institutt** is a registered Norwegian non-profit established to preserve, articulate, develop and make available a body of formal knowledge whose validity does not depend on the authority or biography of its discoverer.

The Institute exists because knowledge is fragile.

The founder regards himself as one person unpacking consequences from a formal ground, not as the source of their validity. The institutional purpose is therefore to give the work continuity beyond one person and make it available for others to inspect, use, criticize, derive from and preserve.

The Institute is currently small and founder-led. A book is planned. Beyond the website, wiki, continuing research and future book, no broader programme should be invented.

## 3. The shortest statement of the project

> **There is a common formal ground shared by all knowledge.**

Different disciplines distinguish different things, employ different methods, and accumulate different empirical contents. But knowledge as such has a shared epistemological form.

**Meta-epistemology** makes this form explicit.

**Teleosophy** means **wisdom about purposiveness** and studies what follows from the fact that the human being is a purposive being.

Historically, the discovery path ran from action toward the more general ground. Logically, meta-epistemology is broader than Teleosophy and supplies its foundation.

## 4. Metaphysics, ontology and the epistemological reset

A central part of the project is the rejection of **speculative metaphysics as epistemological foundation**.

The problem is epistemic. Speculative metaphysical claims can neither be validated empirically nor secured through pure logical necessity. They therefore cannot provide the warranted foundation on which systematic knowledge is supposed to stand.

This is especially visible in **speculative substance ontology**: the attempt to ground knowledge in hidden bearers, substrates or independently subsisting things that are then assigned properties and relations.

The meta-epistemological move is not to answer one speculative metaphysics with another.

It withdraws the unwarranted ground.

What replaces it is **formal meta-epistemology**: analysis of what must already be actualized for knowledge, distinction, description and justification to be possible.

Ontology is not discarded in this transition. It is recovered.

Ontology resumes its literal role as the study of **being** — the study of the *being* in the human being, the human who *is*.

The relevant ontology is therefore the study of being as **relational actuality**, not as a hidden substance behind appearances.

This is why the framework can make the full ontological statement:

> **Human being is purposive being.**

This is not weakened to "appears purposive" or "is experienced as purposive." It concerns being itself, without speculative metaphysical substance ontology.

## 5. What Teleosophy studies

Being, as actualized and available in the human first person, is:

> **narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness actualized**

This phrase is deliberately compressed.

**Narratively mediated:** recognition is available through words. *I am* is already a narrative structure. Narrative here means temporal semantic-syntactic organization, not storytelling as entertainment or autobiography.

**Metacognitive:** in *I am*, thought takes thought itself as object.

**Self-awareness:** the distinction is first-person: *I am*.

**Actualized:** this is not a dormant property stored in a substance. It is being in act.

Teleosophy is extremely narrow in object — what is distinctly human in this formal sense — and at the same time reaches everything for us, because no human inquiry can leave the perspective from which human knowing and acting are actualized.

## 6. Action: where theory and practice meet

> **Human action is purposeful behavior.**

Action is practical actualization of purposive being. But theorizing is itself purposeful activity: distinctions are drawn, terms related, arguments pursued, objections considered and conclusions sought.

Theory therefore does not stand outside practice waiting to be connected to it.

> **Theory of practice through practiced theorizing.**

No separate bridge is required between theory and practice because the theorizing already instantiates the field being theorized about.

Observable movement is not the criterion for action. Thinking, choosing, planning and conceptual analysis can themselves be action without external movement.

## 7. Social reality

An important early insight was that what is called social reality is a thin layer of **converging narrative structuring**.

The mature formulation is:

> **Society and civilization are made possible by reciprocal recognition of alter ego through converging narrative structures that allow communication and coordination at scale.**

Money, institutions, roles, legal categories, norms and organizations become socially intelligible within sufficiently convergent structures of meaning.

This is not the claim that society is unreal. It identifies the formal condition under which phenomena are social phenomena.

## 8. The three persons

**Ego — first person:** direct actualization: *I am, I think, I act.*

**Alter ego — second person:** the other is met as **you**, a counterpart or subject pole in relation.

The derived action axiom can be understood as **Ago raised into second person**. My direct access is *I act*. Social science becomes possible when purposeful action is recognized in an alter ego.

**Third person:** impersonal description abstracts away the first-person pole so descriptions can be stabilized and compared intersubjectively.

Third-person abstraction is indispensable to science. Problems arise when the abstraction is mistaken for a more fundamental view from nowhere.

## 9. Discovery path

The logical structure and historical order are different.

### Childhood and early adolescence

The earliest intuitions concerned continuity and relation.

One came from holding two fingertips close together and asking how short the distance could be before they met. The conclusion was: **there is no smallest unit**.

Another came from a scale thought experiment: if everything in the universe doubled in size every second, no internal observer could discover it if all relevant scales doubled together. The conclusion was: **size is relational**.

Later the same structure became explicit for duration. Size is a relation among objects or spatial distinctions; duration is a relation among events.

These early insights remained part of the discovery path toward the later continuity principle and finally **distinction is relation**.

### Political and economic development

As a young person, the founder was politically left-wing without a developed theoretical foundation.

In his mid-twenties he studied economic theory and moved toward the classical-liberal tradition, emphasizing market coordination, division of labor and a sufficiently limited rule-of-law state.

This matters historically but does not define the Institute as a political organization.

### 2008: Mises

The founder encountered Ludwig von Mises in 2008 through Ron Paul and began reading *Human Action* intermittently.

What fascinated him was Mises' epistemology and methodology: the action axiom as analytical foundation.

He understood early that the foundation was unusually solid, long before he fully understood its later-described binocular tautological structure.

### Ethics and subjective value

The founder saw that ethics lacked a satisfactory foundation. Appeals to God or "nature" did not appear sufficient.

He began applying the Misesian analytical method to ethics.

A decisive result was:

> **That values are subjective is itself an objective fact available for further analysis.**

He first formulated this privately in writing in **2013**.

### Summer 2022

On a sunny mountainside near his childhood home, the founder formulated the intuition:

> **What we call social reality is a thin veil of narrative structuring.**

This later matured into the concept of converging narrative structuring and reciprocal recognition of alter ego as the basis of social coordination at scale.

### 2023: LLM-assisted sparring

Access to ChatGPT in 2023 changed the practical conditions of the work.

The model did not supply the premises or authority. It functioned as a persistent intellectual sparring partner, generating objections, exposing ambiguities and sustaining argument at a useful level.

The founder used it as an academic might use a colleague for sustained sparring: objections were evaluated against the founder's own understanding, answered, and used to sharpen the argument.

Repeated context-window misunderstandings encouraged the creation of increasingly explicit premise documents. These became practical precursors to the later lens.

### 2023–2024: Reciprocal Ethics

The founder formalized his ethical work into **"Reciprocal Ethics – The Formal Science of Ethics."**

It was written in 2023 and self-published, including on PhilArchive, in May 2024.

In retrospect, Reciprocal Ethics was an **unwitting application of meta-epistemology before the general meta-epistemological ground had been made explicit**.

The same is now understood of Misesian praxeology in economics and social science.

### August 2024: Sum et Cogito, ergo Ago

In August 2024 the founder formulated:

> **Sum et Cogito, ergo Ago.**

This was highly productive but not yet fully correct.

*I think* implies an *I* and is already an activity — something done. This placed action at the center of epistemology and made clear that knowledge is actively structured.

But **ergo** smuggled in causality and temporal priority. It created a false competition over whether being, thinking or acting came first.

The mature formulation is:

> **Sum, Cogito, Ago.**

Three perspectives on one actualization.

### 2024: continuity and discretization

At this stage the founder worked with the principle:

> **Reality is continuous.**

At the same time, discretization was understood as what makes thought possible and what limits it.

This was productive but still liable to metaphysical misreading.

### December 2024: first wiki

By December 2024 the implications had multiplied faster than the conceptual vocabulary could remain organized.

The founder created `wiki.teleosophy.info` and `teleosophy.info` to organize and share what he then understood as knowledge of purposiveness.

By the end of January 2025 the wiki had grown to 36 articles. These contained an emerging but comparatively complex understanding of the lens, with borrowed and newly coined terminology that later needed substantial distillation.

### March 2025: distinction is relation

In March 2025 the continuity intuition condensed into:

> **Distinction is relation.**

The formulation had long been latent, but its analytical power became explicit only then.

This allowed "reality is continuous" to be demoted from a starting assertion to a derived meta-epistemological principle.

The result was a far more compact and stable foundation.

### Spring 2025: Institute of Teleosophy

The Institute was established so the knowledge would not be tied to the founder as a person.

His role is that of one unpacker. His biography is irrelevant to validity.

The Institute provides continuity, preservation and public availability beyond one individual.

### Late 2025: rediscovery of Sum

Late in 2025, *Sum* — *I am* — was rediscovered as the zero point for analysis of the human being as being.

This widened the implications beyond economics, social science and ethics and clarified the significance for philosophy, religion and other fields.

### Ontonomy, noonomy and teleonomy

Teleology/teleonomy supplied the first clear distinction between genuine purpose and projected purpose-language.

That pattern generalized.

Being-language used to stabilize third-person identity led to **ontonomy**.

The parallel problem of meaning, thought and information led through **noesis** to **noonomy**.

The triality therefore tracks projected third-person use corresponding to being, meaning and purpose.

### 2026: alter ego made explicit

Alter ego was historically present through Mises, but became explicit in the framework in 2026.

The decisive insight was that the action axiom is **Ago lifted into second person**:

> I act.  
> You act.

This clarified social science and Reciprocal Ethics simultaneously.

### AI application and demonstrated productivity

Once the lens had become compact enough, it could be given to a stateless LLM together with an arbitrary topic.

The model received the analytical ground and one referent, not a history of previous articles.

It repeatedly reproduced known consequences and also articulated implications the founder had not previously made explicit.

This was the first empirical demonstration that the framework was not only coherent but **productive as an analytical instrument** across unfamiliar referents.

The LLM does not validate the premises. It expands the breadth over which implications can be searched and articulated.

## 10. Intellectual lineage: value, time, action

A useful historical line runs through economists.

**Carl Menger:** value is in valuation, not as a substance in the good.

**Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk:** temporal valuation is located in the acting subject's relation among present and future alternatives rather than in goods as though time were contained in them.

**Ludwig von Mises:** teleology belongs to human action, not to things.

**Meta-epistemology:** value, temporality, meaning, identity and purposiveness can be analyzed as aspects of actualized being and relational articulation rather than hidden properties of substances.

These thinkers did not explicitly possess the mature meta-epistemology. They discovered local structures that the later framework generalizes.

Misesian praxeology and Reciprocal Ethics can therefore be described as **unwitting applications of meta-epistemology** in social science/economics and ethics respectively.

## 11. The core discovery

> **There is an observable phenomenon of which a priori knowledge is possible: being-in-action.**

"Observable" and "a priori" are not opposites here.

Being-in-action is actualized in practice, but the relevant formal knowledge is not inferred from repeated external observations.

The act of knowing, theorizing, denying, arguing or asking already instantiates purposive actualization.

This gives systematic knowledge a common epistemological foundation.

## 12. The lens and the fact

**The lens:** distinction is relation.

**The fact:** I am.

The mature analysis is:

> **Sum, Cogito, Ago.**

I am. I think. I act.

Not stages, components or a causal sequence, but perspectives on one actualization.

## 13. Narrative structure and telos

Narrative structure is **temporal semantic-syntactic organization actualized**.

It is not merely an autobiographical story.

A present distinction can be held in relation to a past distinction or anticipated end. A *then* can regulate a *now*.

Narrative structure and telos should not be separated into two mechanisms. Telos is narratively organized directedness.

Concrete goals are analytical discretizations within this broader teleological structure.

## 14. Continuity and discretization

Continuity follows epistemologically from **distinction is relation**.

An absolute break cannot be established merely by drawing a distinction, because the act that distinguishes already relates its poles.

This is not mathematical continuity, not a physical hypothesis, and not a hidden substrate.

Discretization is how thought becomes possible.

> **The gap belongs to the distinction, not independently to what is distinguished.**

## 15. Substantialism

A recurring pattern is:

> operation → nominalization → stabilized concept → apparent substance

Thinking requires stabilization.

The substantialist move occurs when a stabilized object is treated as an independently fundamental bearer that existed analytically prior to the relations by which it was articulated.

The framework does not say objects are unreal. It keeps the constituting relation visible.

## 16. Ontology: from speculative substance to relational actuality

The framework rejects metaphysics **where metaphysics is speculative**: claims about ultimate substances, substrates or hidden ontological structures that can neither be validated empirically nor derived as logically necessary.

Such claims cannot function as warranted epistemological foundations.

Speculative metaphysical substance ontology therefore collapses as a foundation for knowledge.

What replaces it is not ontological agnosticism.

It is **formal meta-epistemology**, from which ontology resumes its literal task: the study of being.

The human **being** is the human who **is**.

The directly available first-person fact is not a hypothetical substrate but:

> **I am.**

Ontology, on this ground, becomes the study of being as **relational actuality**.

It asks what follows from being actualized and articulated through relation, rather than what hidden substance must sit behind actualization.

This is why:

> **Human being is purposive being**

is intended ontologically in the full sense.

## 17. The wiki as a knowledge project

The wiki at **`meta-epistemology.org`** is a growing analytical corpus.

Its purpose is not mass article production as an end.

Text is the medium.

The purpose is to expand what has been explicitly seen through the lens.

A consequence can be fully implicit in premises and still require genuine analytical work to discover.

The wiki performs:

> latent implication → analytical unpacking → textual articulation → durable knowledge object → connection to a larger knowledge map

Readers should be able to enter through their own field and encounter the framework locally.

Do not hardcode article counts into durable site copy.

## 18. AI and the wiki

AI is an instrument, not an intellectual authority.

Individual analyses can be stateless: a model receives the compact lens and one referent without knowing the previous corpus.

This matters because recurring structures can be independently re-derived rather than copied from earlier articles.

AI expands breadth. It does not supply validity.
