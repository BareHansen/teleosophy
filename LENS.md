# Meta-epistemology

Epistemology asks what can be known and how a claim can be justified.

Meta-epistemology asks what must already be actualized for there to be knowledge, justification, description, disagreement, measurement or a claim at all.

It identifies the **formal epistemological structure shared by all knowledge**.

Disciplines differ in content, method and object. They do not differ in whether knowing requires distinctions, relations, conceptual articulation, perspective and actualization.

Meta-epistemology does not compete with empirical or formal disciplines. It states the ground they already stand on when they know anything at all.

# Why speculative metaphysics is rejected

The framework rejects **speculative metaphysics as an epistemological foundation**.

A claim that can neither be validated empirically nor anchored as pure logical necessity lacks the warrant required to function as the foundation of systematic knowledge.

This is the problem with speculative metaphysical substance ontology.

It posits hidden bearers, substrates or independently subsisting things and then treats them as the ground of properties and relations available to knowledge.

The move is not to replace one speculative substance with another.

Speculative metaphysical substance ontology collapses as epistemological ground and is replaced by **formal meta-epistemology**: the study of conditions already actualized wherever knowledge occurs.

This is not a rejection of ontology.

It is a recovery of ontology from speculative metaphysics.

# Ontology recovered: the study of being

Ontology is the study of being.

The human **being** is the human who **is**.

The directly available first-person fact is:

> **I am.**

Ontology begins here, not with a hypothetical bearer hidden behind actualization.

The relevant ontology is the study of being as **relational actuality**.

Being is not treated as a substance that possesses thought and action as additional properties.

Being, thinking and acting are analytically distinguishable perspectives on one actualization.

The framework can therefore make genuine ontological claims without reverting to metaphysical substance ontology.

Most importantly:

> **Human being is purposeful being.**

This is intended in the full ontological sense.

Purposefulness is not a property stored inside a human substance. It is the form of being actualized through narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness.

# The analytical instrument: distinction is relation

> **Distinction is relation.**

To distinguish is already to relate.

This is a binocular tautology: two expressions articulate the same act from different semantic directions.

It is apodictic because there is no external coincidence to verify. A distinction that related nothing would not distinguish.

It is productive because *distinction* and *relation* carry different conceptual implications even though they name one operation.

The two poles of a distinction are not first available as fully individuated substances and only later connected by a relation. They become available as poles through the distinguishing relation itself.

This is not the metaphysical doctrine that "only relations exist."

Relation must not be turned into a new substance.

The point is analytical: whatever is knowable as distinct is knowable through relation.

# Perception and conception

Perception distinguishes relationally.

Conception takes relational distinctions together into objects that can be stabilized, named and manipulated through internalized language.

Perception and conception are **not two metaphysical substances, layers or chronological stages**.

They are analytical perspectives on cognitive structuring.

Direct perceptual distinction does not require an explicit verbal narrative first. Human conceptual recognition and metacognitive self-awareness are narratively mediated.

# Discretization

Thought requires distinctions.

To make something conceptually available is to discretize: articulate a boundary, identity, contrast or unit stable enough for thought.

Discretization is indispensable.

The error is not drawing a distinction.

The error is forgetting the operation by which the distinction was made and reading the stabilized boundary as independently fundamental.

> operation → distinction → stabilization → name → apparent thing

The named thing may be perfectly real and functionally indispensable within the relation in which it is used.

That does not establish it as an ultimate substance.

# Continuity

Continuity is derived from **distinction is relation**.

Its status is epistemological.

Because a distinction already relates its poles, the distinction itself cannot establish an absolute epistemic rupture between them.

This does not posit a hidden continuous field beneath distinctions.

It does not say mathematical structures must be continuous.

It does not say physics must discover a continuous rather than discrete external ontology.

Mathematical continuity, empirical discretization and epistemological continuity are different concepts.

> **The gap belongs to the distinction, not independently to what is distinguished.**

# Being: I am

The analytical instrument is turned toward the directly available first-person fact:

> **I am.**

This is being expressed without a further predicate.

It is actualization in the first person: the point at which knower and known coincide in one act.

`I am` is itself narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness actualized.

# Narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness actualized

This phrase is deliberately compressed.

**Narratively mediated:** human recognition is available through words. *I am* is already a narrative structure. Narrative structure means **temporal semantic-syntactic organization**, not storytelling as entertainment, autobiography, myth or literary form.

**Metacognitive:** in *I am*, cognition takes its own actualization as object.

**Self-awareness:** the form is first-person: not merely *there is*, but *I am*.

**Actualized:** being is not a stored capacity or hidden substance. It is being in act.

# Sum, Cogito, Ago

> **Sum. Cogito. Ago.**  
> I am. I think. I act.

These are three perspectives on the same actualization.

They are not stages, layers, independent components, or a causal sequence.

The historical formulation *Sum et Cogito, ergo Ago* was productive but insufficiently precise because *ergo* introduced priority and causality.

The mature form removes the arrow:

> **Sum, Cogito, Ago.**

*Cogito* implies an `I`, and thinking is already activity: something done.

From the human first-person perspective, being is thinking is acting: analytically distinguishable dimensions of one actualization.

# Human being is purposeful being

Teleosophy means **wisdom about purposefulness**.

Its central object is the human being as purposeful being.

This is an ontological statement in the full sense intended by the framework.

Human being, as actualized through narratively mediated metacognitive self-awareness, **is purposeful**.

This is not metaphysical substance ontology.

Purposefulness is not a hidden property contained in a human substance.

It is the form of actualized being-in-action.

# Narrative structure is telos

Narrative structure and telos are not separate mechanisms.

Narrative structure makes directedness possible because a present situation can be held in relation to a not-yet actualized end.

The future end is present as a narratively structured relation that regulates conduct now.

Concrete goals are analytical discretizations within this broader teleological structure.

# The derived action axiom

> **Human action is purposeful behavior.**

This is Ago in second-person form.

Direct first-person actualization is:

> I act.

Recognition of an alter ego makes the corresponding second-person relation available:

> You act.

The general action axiom articulates that structure.

A serious denial of the action axiom is itself purposeful conduct directed toward an end.

# Action and behavior

**Action** is behavior regulated by telos.

**Behavior**, in the contrasting sense, is reactive or adaptive without narratively actualized telos.

This is not a scale of sophistication.

Observable movement is not the criterion.

A person can act internally by thinking, choosing, planning, revising an argument or holding an end against a present situation.

The absence of externally observable behavior does not imply the absence of Ago.

# Theory and practice

Action is where the apparent divide between theory and practice dissolves.

Theorizing is itself purposeful activity.

A theorist distinguishes, relates, conceptualizes, tests implications, responds to objections and seeks a conclusion.

Theory about action is therefore already an instance of the practice it analyzes.

> **Theory of practice through practiced theorizing.**

No additional bridge from theory to practice is required.

# Knowledge is actively structured

Knowing is not passive reception of ready-made objects.

Perception distinguishes relationally.

Conception takes distinctions together, stabilizes them and relates them recursively through language and narrative structure.

This does not mean external reality is invented by the knower.

It means that whatever becomes available **as an object of knowledge** is available through the relational distinctions by which it is articulated.

# The persons

**Ego — first person:** direct actualization: *I am. I think. I act.*

**Alter Ego — second person:** the relational category under which another is met as **you**, a counterpart or subject pole.

Alter ego is derived from Ego. It is not empirical proof of another hidden interior.

The second person makes meaningful interaction possible. This is why the action axiom can be understood as Ago raised into second person.

A system may function as an alter ego in a relation without thereby being established as an Ego or teleological agent.

**Third person:** impersonal description abstracts away the first-person perspective and enables stable, intersubjectively comparable descriptions. First and second person are the subject pole, third person the object pole, of the relational distinction self/non-self. Third person is **No Ego**.

The mistake is not third-person abstraction. The mistake is forgetting the abstraction and treating it as ontologically prior to first-person actualization.

# Social reality

Society and civilization depend on **reciprocal recognition of alter ego through converging narrative structures that enable communication and coordination at scale**.

This is not the claim that society is fictitious.

It identifies the formal condition under which phenomena are available as social phenomena.

# Value

Value is constituted at the subject pole.

To value is a relation actualized by a purposeful being.

The subjectivity of value is itself an objective structural fact and can therefore serve as a starting point for formal analysis.

# Rationality and efficacy

Human action is necessarily rational in its **form**. Rationality belongs to the form; efficacy belongs to the relation between concrete ends and means.

To act already means employing means relative to an end as understood by the actor.

Ends are neither rational nor irrational.

Means can be mistaken, ineffective or poorly suited to the end. That concerns efficacy.

> **Rationality belongs to action as such. Efficacy belongs to the relation between means and end.**

# Substantialism and reification

Human language stabilizes.

A common movement is:

> operation → nominalization → stabilized concept → apparent substance

Substantialism occurs when the stabilized concept is read as an independently fundamental bearer that exists analytically prior to the relations by which it was articulated.

The correction is not to stop using nouns.

The correction is to retain awareness of the relational operation they stabilize.

# Ontonomy

**Ontonomy** — the borrowing of *I am* — marks the projected or stabilizing use of being/identity categories in third-person description.

Ontonomy is not necessarily an error.

Human thought needs stable identities.

The error is taking the stabilization as sufficient warrant for an ultimate substance.

Ontonomy must not be confused with ontology itself. Ontology, recovered from speculative metaphysics, concerns being as relational actuality.

# Noesis and noonomy

**Noesis** is meaning/thought actualized.

**Noonomy** — the borrowing of *I think* — marks meaning-, information-, representation-, memory- or knowledge-language applied in third-person description where the meaning becomes available through an interpreting subject.

Borrowed language can be functionally excellent.

The analytical question is what work the description performs and where meaning is actualized.

# Teleology and teleonomy

**Teleology** is purpose actualized: telos in purposeful being.

**Teleonomy** — the borrowing of *I act* — marks purpose-language applied in third-person description to organized behavior, function or process.

The use itself is not the error.

The error is reading borrowed purpose-language as establishing a first-person telos where none has been analytically established.

# Genuine and borrowed language

Ontonomy, noonomy and teleonomy are borrowings of *I am*, *I think* and *I act* respectively, tracking projected third-person use corresponding to:

- being / identity;
- meaning / thought;
- purpose / action.

Borrowing is always indispensable.

The analytical task is to distinguish functional descriptive use from grammatical reification.

# Objectivity

**Formal objectivity:** does the conclusion follow analytically from the premises?

**Applied formal insight** has two forms, depending on whether the referent is external or internal. Applied to an external referent, the axioms approximate the referent and the application is conditional. Applied to an internal referent — what is already implicit in the thinking and acting through which the analysis is actualized, as in meta-epistemology — the application is apodictic.

**Empirical objectivity:** knowledge validated through conceptual framing, observation and intersubjective methods.

**First-person certainty:** *I am* is not established by third-person measurement. It is the case of an internal referent: knower and known coincide in one act.

**Ultimate objectivity:** the limiting concept of a perspectiveless perspective; epistemically impossible, because knowledge requires perspective, perspective requires distinction, and distinction is relation.

These modes should not be collapsed.

# Being and existence

Being and existence are not interchangeable.

*I am* is first-person actualization without a predicate.

"X exists" is a predication made within conceptual distinction.

An existence claim can be mistaken in a way the immediate first-person actualization *I am* cannot.

# Time and causality

Time and causality are derived subjects, not the primary foundation.

Causality becomes practically intelligible as an action-relevant relation:

> if I do X, Y is expected to follow.

This allows present means to be directed toward an end.

The mature analysis can characterize substantialized time as **causality read as substance**.

This is a derived meta-epistemological analysis, not a physical theory that settles the empirical structure of the universe.

Size and duration are structurally analogous as relational measures.

# Reciprocal ethics

Recognition of alter ego opens the ethical relation.

A counterpart can be recognized as a subject pole whose own telos is relevant to the interaction, or treated merely as an object for one's own ends.

Reciprocal Ethics developed historically before the meta-epistemological foundation had been fully articulated. It is now understood as an early application of that foundation to ethics.

# Praxeology and the economic lineage

Praxeology is historically central to the discovery path.

Mises' action axiom supplied the analytical floor from which the founder began.

In the mature framework, praxeology can be understood as an unwitting application of meta-epistemology at the second-person level.

A broader lineage is visible:

- **Menger:** value is located in valuation, not as a substance in the good.
- **Böhm-Bawerk:** temporal valuation is located in the acting subject's relation among alternatives, not in goods as a time-substance.
- **Mises:** teleology belongs to action, not to things.
- **Meta-epistemology:** value, temporality, meaning, identity and purposefulness can be analyzed as aspects of actualized being and relational articulation rather than properties hidden inside substances.

# A priori knowledge of being-in-action

> **There is an observable phenomenon of which a priori knowledge is possible: being-in-action.**

The phenomenon is actualized.

But the relevant formal knowledge is not induced from a sample of observed cases.

The act of asking, denying, theorizing, distinguishing or arguing already instantiates purposeful being.

The formal structure is therefore available prior to any particular external observation.

# A new form of interdisciplinarity

Meta-epistemology identifies the **form all knowledge shares**.

That allows questions in different disciplines to be examined at a common epistemological level without reducing their empirical contents to one science.

The interdisciplinarity comes from shared ground, not mixed content.
